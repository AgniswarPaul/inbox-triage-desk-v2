# Northwind Goods Returns Policy

## Return window
- Items can be returned within 30 days from delivery.
- Items must be unworn, unused, and in their original condition with tags attached.
- Requests made more than 30 days after delivery are outside the return window and are declined. Decline politely and state the 30-day window.

## How to return
- For eligible returns, the customer replies to this email to receive a prepaid return label. Once the item is received and inspected, a refund goes to the original payment method.
- Refunds never exceed the amount paid for the order.
- Support replies must not say a return or refund has been processed or issued.

## Damaged or defective items
- If an item arrived damaged or defective, a team member reviews photos of the damage before offering a replacement or refund. These cases always go to a human.

## Out of scope
- Exchanges for a different item and returns of cancelled orders are not covered by this policy.
