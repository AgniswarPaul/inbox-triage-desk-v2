# Northwind Goods Billing Policy

## Duplicate charges
- If a customer reports being charged more than once for the same order, compare the amount charged with the order amount.
- If the amount charged is higher than the order amount, the billing team verifies the duplicate charge with the payment processor.
- After verification, the duplicate charge is refunded to the original payment method. Refunds appear within 5 to 7 business days after verification.
- Support replies must not say a refund has been issued or processed. Say the billing team will verify the charge and refund the duplicate once confirmed.

## Pending authorizations
- A pending authorization is a temporary hold, not a charge. Holds for cancelled orders drop off within 3 to 5 business days.

## Out of scope
- Price adjustments, discounts, or refunds not tied to a verified billing error are not covered by this policy.
