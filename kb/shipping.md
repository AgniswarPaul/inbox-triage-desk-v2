# Northwind Goods Shipping Policy

## Processing
- Orders ship within 1 to 2 business days of being placed. Orders with status "processing" have not shipped yet and have no tracking number.

## Tracking
- Once an order ships, the customer can track it with the carrier and tracking number on the order. Always include the tracking number in the reply when the order has one.
- Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days after shipping.

## Delays
- Orders with status "delayed" are held up by the carrier. Share the tracking number and let the customer know we are monitoring it.
- If an order is more than 5 business days past its expected delivery, the team opens a lost-package investigation with the carrier.

## Out of scope
- Changing the delivery address after shipping and international customs questions are not covered by this policy.
